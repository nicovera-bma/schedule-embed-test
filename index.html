What to check on each page

  1. Click Show more two or three times. The widget should grow, with no inner scrollbar and nothing cut off.
  2. Watch the host page: it should not jump or scroll by itself.
  3. Open Full Calendar, and on a phone the Filter dialog. Both should open where you can see and close them.
  4. On a phone or tablet, turn it to landscape and repeat.
